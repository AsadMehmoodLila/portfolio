## Jurisdiction and regulatory assessment

- **Pakistan/Punjab (provisional):** the official Ministry of IT legislation list still identifies the Personal Data Protection Bill May 2023 as a draft. Do not state that a draft is binding law or infer that no privacy duties exist. This source is not a complete gazette check; a Pakistan-qualified lawyer should confirm current enactments and rules if collecting data or selling services. [Official legislation list](https://www.moitt.gov.pk/Legislations).
- **Consumer protection:** the official Punjab legal-framework index lists the Punjab Consumer Protection Act 2005 and links to the statute. The statute page could not be fetched during this audit, so specific disclosure/refund clauses are not asserted. No online sale is currently present. Review provincial consumer law, business identification, cancellation/refund duties and tax/contract requirements before future paid services or platform sales. [Official Punjab index](https://multan.punjab.gov.pk/legal_framework).
- **Electronic transactions:** Pakistan's Electronic Transactions Ordinance 2002 is an official legal starting point for electronic records and agreements; it does not make a generic website policy an enforceable paid-service contract. [Official Pakistan Code](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/english/UY2FqaJw2-apaUY2Fqa-apaUY2Fta5Y%3D-con-10070-sg-jjjjjjjjjjjjj).
- **EU/UK reach:** GDPR Article 3 considers establishment, offering goods/services to EU individuals and monitoring their behaviour. Mere global access does not establish applicability on its own. Targeted services or profiling require a new scope assessment. [GDPR official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng). UK ICO guidance distinguishes optional from strictly necessary storage. In this version there is no optional storage to consent to. [ICO guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/).
- **US accessibility:** DOJ guidance addresses state/local government and businesses open to the public. No US establishment or covered entity has been established for this individual portfolio. WCAG 2.2 AA is the engineering target, not an automatic ADA safe harbor or guarantee. [DOJ guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/), [W3C WCAG quick reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/).
